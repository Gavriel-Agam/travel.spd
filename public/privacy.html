Privacy Policy

SmartTrip (formerly TravelSpend Couple) · Last updated: November 19, 2026

SmartTrip (formerly TravelSpend Couple) is a free web app for tracking your travel expenses, with the option to share a trip with a partner. This page explains what information the app stores and how it is used.

Information we store

  • Account details: your email address and, if you sign in with Google or Facebook, your name and profile picture as provided by that service. If your Facebook account has no email address, you can still sign in with Facebook, and no email address is stored for you. We use Google and Facebook sign-in only to identify you. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data, and we do not post to Facebook or see your friends, posts or any other Facebook data.
  • Trip data you enter: trips, expenses, amounts, currencies, categories (with any picture you upload for a category, stored as a small icon), the order of the trip's categories, payment methods you add to a trip, budgets, the trip's destination countries (if you set them), the foreign transaction fee percentage of your own card (if you set it; each person on a trip has their own, and the people on that trip can see it), the country each expense was in (its location), and whether it was bought tax free (used to estimate a VAT refund inside the app), notes, receipt photos, trip cover photos and the profile photo you may add to yourself on a trip (shown to the people on that trip).
  • App preferences: language, which cards show on your Home screen and their order (including cards you hid), and whether your account was already offered the feedback panel (so it opens by itself only once). The order you arrange a day's expenses in, the currency, payment method, location, who paid and how it was split that you used last (so a new expense starts with them), and whether each Home card shows the whole trip or your share of it, are kept on your device only.
  • Smart import use: each time you use Smart import, a record with your account and the time, used only for the limit of 15 imports in 24 hours. Records older than 2 days are deleted the next time you use it. The text and screenshots you send are not stored by us. If you turn on "Save screenshots as receipts" before adding the expenses, each screenshot is stored once as a receipt photo of the expenses found in it, like any other receipt photo.
  • Feedback you send: the text you write in the feedback panel, linked to your account and, if you send it from a trip, to that trip, with your app language and version.
  • Error reports: when something goes wrong, the app records the error message, the page it happened on, your browser type (user agent) and the app version, linked to your account, so we can fix bugs.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, to save and sync your trip with the partner you invited, to calculate exchange rates, budgets and settle-up, to read expenses from text or screenshots you choose to send with Smart import, and to fix errors. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.

Who can see your data

Trips are private. Only you and the partner who joined your trip with its invite code can see that trip's data. For each expense, the app shows the people on the trip who added it and who changed it last, with the time.

One exception inside a trip: a personal expense that its owner paid for themself (split set to Personal, paid by the same person) is visible only to that person. Other trip members don't see it, its receipt photo or its amount, and it is not counted in their totals. The "Your share" total on the Home screen, made of your personal expenses, is shown only to you. A personal expense that someone else paid for you stays visible to both of you, because it is a debt between you.

You can send the invite on WhatsApp from Settings. The app then opens WhatsApp with a message you send yourself: the trip's name, a link to join and the invite code. We send nothing to WhatsApp. Anyone who has the code can use it to join the trip, so share it only with your partner. If the link is opened before signing in, the code is kept on that device until sign-in, then removed.

The person who created a trip can remove the partner from it. A removed partner can no longer see the trip. Their name and the expenses they already added stay in the trip, so totals and balances remain correct. If they are invited again, they get access back.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, which provides the app's database, sign-in and file storage. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Exchange rates are fetched from public rate services; no personal information is sent to them. When you send feedback, its text (without your name, email or trip) is passed to ntfy.sh, a notification service, so the developer gets an alert on their phone or by email. When you use Smart import, the text and screenshots you add, together with the trip's currency, the names of the people on the trip and the category names, are sent to Google's Gemini API to find the expenses in them; Google processes them under its Gemini API terms. Your email address is not sent. A copy of your trip data is kept on your device for up to a month so the app works without a connection, together with a copy of the Home screen as it was last shown, so the app opens instantly (deleted when you sign out). Changes you make offline (new, edited, moved or deleted expenses, and changes to the trip's categories, payment methods, details, budget, and your name and card fee) are kept there until they sync. A Smart import that is waiting (its text, screenshots made smaller on your device, and the expenses found) is kept on your device until you add its expenses or delete it, including one saved while offline, which is sent when the connection returns.

Deleting your data

You can delete expenses in the app at any time. A deleted expense disappears from the app for both partners, but it is not erased: it stays stored, marked as deleted, together with its receipt photo if it had one. The person who created a trip can delete the whole trip in the app (Settings, swipe the trip): this erases it for everyone in it, permanently, with all its expenses (deleted ones too), receipt photos, cover photo, member photos, categories, budgets and invite codes. Feedback sent from that trip is kept, without the link to the trip. To delete your account and all of its data, including deleted expenses and their photos, email us at the address below and we will delete it.

מי רואה את המידע

הטיולים פרטיים. רק את/ה ובן או בת הזוג שהצטרפו לטיול עם קוד ההזמנה יכולים לראות את נתוני הטיול. בכל הוצאה האפליקציה מציגה לאנשים בטיול מי הוסיף אותה ומי ערך אותה לאחרונה, עם השעה.

יוצא מן הכלל בתוך הטיול: הוצאה אישית ששולמה על ידי מי שהיא שייכת לו (חלוקה "אישי", ומי ששילם הוא אותו אדם) גלויה רק לו. שאר משתתפי הטיול לא רואים אותה, את צילום הקבלה שלה או את הסכום שלה, והיא לא נספרת בסכומים שלהם. הסכום "החלק שלך" במסך הבית, שמורכב מההוצאות האישיות שלך, מוצג רק לך. הוצאה אישית שמישהו אחר שילם עבורך נשארת גלויה לשניכם, כי היא חוב ביניכם.

אפשר לשלוח את ההזמנה בוואטסאפ מההגדרות. האפליקציה פותחת את וואטסאפ עם הודעה שאת/ה שולחים בעצמכם: שם הטיול, קישור להצטרפות וקוד ההזמנה. אנחנו לא שולחים דבר לוואטסאפ. כל מי שיש לו את הקוד יכול להצטרף איתו לטיול, לכן שתפו אותו רק עם בן או בת הזוג. אם הקישור נפתח לפני התחברות, הקוד נשמר במכשיר עד ההתחברות ואז נמחק.

מי שיצר/ה את הטיול יכול/ה להסיר ממנו את בן או בת הזוג. מי שהוסר/ה לא יכול/ה לראות יותר את הטיול. השם וההוצאות שכבר הוסיפו נשארים בטיול, כדי שהסכומים וההתחשבנות יישארו נכונים. אם יוזמנו שוב, הגישה תחזור.
