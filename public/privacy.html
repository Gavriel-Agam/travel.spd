Privacy Policy

SmartTrip (formerly TravelSpend Couple) · Last updated: November 10, 2026

SmartTrip (formerly TravelSpend Couple) is a free web app for tracking your travel expenses, with the option to share a trip with a partner. This page explains what information the app stores and how it is used.

Information we store

  • Account details: your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name and profile picture as provided by Google. We use Google sign-in only to identify you. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Trip data you enter: trips, expenses, amounts, currencies, categories (with any picture you upload for a category, stored as a small icon), the order of the trip's categories, payment methods you add to a trip, budgets, notes, receipt photos, trip cover photos and the profile photo you may add to yourself on a trip (shown to the people on that trip).
  • App preferences: language, the order of cards on your Home screen, and whether your account was already offered the feedback panel (so it opens by itself only once).
  • Feedback you send: the text you write in the feedback panel, linked to your account and, if you send it from a trip, to that trip, with your app language and version.
  • Error reports: when something goes wrong, the app records the error message, the page it happened on, your browser type (user agent) and the app version, linked to your account, so we can fix bugs.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, to save and sync your trip with the partner you invited, to calculate exchange rates, budgets and settle-up, and to fix errors. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.

Who can see your data

Trips are private. Only you and the partner who joined your trip with its invite code can see that trip's data.

The person who created a trip can remove the partner from it. A removed partner can no longer see the trip. Their name and the expenses they already added stay in the trip, so totals and balances remain correct. If they are invited again, they get access back.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, which provides the app's database, sign-in and file storage. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Exchange rates are fetched from public rate services; no personal information is sent to them. When you send feedback, its text (without your name, email or trip) is passed to ntfy.sh, a notification service, so the developer gets an alert on their phone or by email. A copy of your trip data is kept on your device for up to a month so the app works without a connection, and changes you make offline (new, edited or deleted expenses) are kept there until they sync.

Deleting your data

You can delete expenses in the app at any time. A deleted expense disappears from the app for both partners, but it is not erased: it stays stored, marked as deleted, together with its receipt photo if it had one. To delete your account and all of its data, including deleted expenses and their photos, email us at the address below and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
