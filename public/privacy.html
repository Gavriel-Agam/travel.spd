Privacy Policy

TravelSpend Couple (Travel.Spd) · Last updated: November 7, 2026

TravelSpend Couple is a free web app that lets two partners log and share travel expenses. This page explains what information the app stores and how it is used.

Information we store

  • Account details: your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name and profile picture as provided by Google. We use Google sign-in only to identify you. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Trip data you enter: trips, expenses, amounts, currencies, categories, budgets, notes, receipt photos and trip cover photos.
  • App preferences: language and the order of cards on your Home screen.
  • Error reports: when something goes wrong, the app records the error message, the page it happened on, your browser type (user agent) and the app version, linked to your account, so we can fix bugs.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, to save and sync your trip with the partner you invited, to calculate exchange rates, budgets and settle-up, and to fix errors. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.

Who can see your data

Trips are private. Only you and the partner who joined your trip with its invite code can see that trip's data.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, which provides the app's database, sign-in and file storage. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Exchange rates are fetched from public rate services; no personal information is sent to them. Expenses you add while offline are kept on your device until they sync.

Deleting your data

You can delete expenses in the app at any time. To delete your account and all of its data, email us at the address below and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
