The service

SmartTrip (formerly TravelSpend Couple) is a free app for logging your travel expenses, with the option to share a trip with a partner. It is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind, and may change or stop at any time.

Your account

You can sign in with an email and password, or with Google or Facebook; signing in with Google or Facebook is also subject to that service's own terms. You are responsible for the content you enter and for keeping access to your account secure. Share your trip's invite code only with the person you want to join the trip. The person who created a trip can delete it, which deletes it for everyone in it and cannot be undone.

Exchange rates and amounts

Exchange rates come from public sources and may differ from the rates your bank or card charged. Totals, budgets and settle-up amounts are for your convenience only and are not financial advice.

Smart import

Smart import reads expenses from text, screenshots and files (spreadsheets and documents, read on your device) automatically and can make mistakes in amounts, currencies, dates, categories or who paid. Check every expense before adding it. Up to 15 imports per person in 24 hours.

Brand names and logos in the category and brand bank belong to their owners and are shown only to label your categories. SmartTrip is not affiliated with those brands.

Automatic import and card statements

Payments your iPhone Shortcuts send, and lines read from a card statement, are read automatically and can be wrong or missed (for example when a card company changes its message format). They wait under "Pending review" until you confirm them; check each one. Shortcuts are an Apple app that you set up yourself: we don't control when they run. Your personal key works like a password: keep it private and revoke it in Settings if a phone is lost. Each key can be used up to 60 times an hour.
